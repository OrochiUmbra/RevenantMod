# Grimora Mod Revenant Card Pack
A Grimora Mod Expansion Card Pack consists of mainly cards that return to the player in a variety of ways. (P03 KCM Support)<br/>

<details>
<summary>26 New Sigil(s):
</summary>

|Name			| Description	|
|-----------------------|---------------|
|Bad Omen		| At the end of the owner's turn, a card bearing this sigil will deal 1 damage to the owner.|
|Calamity Effusion	| At the end of the owner's turn, all creatures on the board lose 1 health.|
|Comfort Aura		| At the end of the owner's turn, creatures adjacent to a card bearing this sigil heal 1 health.|
|Corpse Party		| When a non-brittle creature on the owner's side perishes by combat, a Zombie enters your hand. A Zombie is defined as: 1 Power, 1 Health.|
|Death Knock		| When a card bearing this sigil perishes by combat, it strikes back then perishes.|
|Death Metal		| When a card bearing this sigil is played, adjacent creatures and opposing creatures to the left, right, and center of it take 1 damage. It gains 1 Power for each affected creature.|
|Defiler		| When a card bearing this sigil perishes, draw a card then choose one to discard from your hand.|
|Encroachment		| A card bearing this sigil creates a copy of it in your hand after 2 turns on the board.|
|Exorcise		| When a card bearing this sigil is played, Bones equal to the Bone cost of the opposing creature are granted to the player.|
|Exploding Corpse	| When a card bearing this sigil perishes by combat, all empty spaces on the board are filled with a Guts card.|
|Grim Epitaph		| When a card bearing this sigil is played, you may choose a card that has already been put to rest, to bring back to your hand.|
|Grudge Collector	| A card bearing this sigil gains 1 power for every 2 times opponent creatures deal direct damage to its owner.|
|Ignition		| A card bearing this sigil gains 1 power at the cost of 1 health after each turn on the board.|
|Limbo			| When a card bearing this sigil perishes, it returns to the top of your deck.|
|Necrosis		| A card bearing this sigil loses 1 power and 1 max health after each turn on the board.|
|Macabre Bone Pulse	| When a card bearing this sigil is played, it perishes immediately, dropping Bones equal to double the amount of creatures on the owner's side.|
|Maternal		| Creatures adjacent to a card bearing this sigil are inscrybed by Waterborne.|
|Parting Stroke		| When a non-brittle creature on the owner's side perishes by combat, its owner chooses a creature to lose all of its sigils.|
|Protector		| Creatures adjacent to a card bearing this sigil gain 1 max health.
|Rancor			| When a card bearing this sigil perishes, it leaves a permanent 1 power buff in the space it occupied.|
|Relentless Revenant	| At the start of the owner's turn, creatures adjacent to a card bearing this sigil transform into a Revenant. A Revenant is defined as: 3 Power, 1 Health, Brittle.|
|Revedue		| After attacking, a card bearing this sigil perishes, then shuffle 2 Revenants with its old sigils into your side deck. A Revenant is defined as: 3 Power, 1 Health, Brittle.|
|Skeletonize		| When a card bearing this sigil perishes, a Skeleton with its old sigils enters your hand. A Skeleton is defined as: 1 Power, 1 Health, Brittle.|
|Soothing Meadows	| At the end of the owner's turn, all creatures on the board gain 1 health.|
|Splitting Corpse	| When a card bearing this sigil perishes by combat, create a Dysmorphia on each empty adjacent space.|
|Torpid			| A card bearing this sigil cannot attack normally.|
</details>

<details>
<summary>5 New Latch Sigil(s):
</summary>

|Name			|Description	|
|-----------------------|---------------|
|Digger Latch		| At the end of the owner's turn, a card bearing this sigil will generate 1 Bone. When it perishes, its owner chooses a creature to gain the Bone Digger sigil.|
|Primed Digger Latch	| At the end of the owner's turn, a card bearing this sigil will generate 1 Bone. When it perishes, its owner chooses a creature to gain this sigil.|
|Ignite			| When a card bearing this sigil perishes, its owner chooses a creature to gain the Ignition sigil.|
|Rejuvenation		| When a card bearing this sigil perishes, its owner chooses a creature to gain the Fledgling sigil.|
|Skull Storm		| At the end of the owner's turn, this sigil is replaced with another sigil at random. When it perishes, its owner chooses a creature to gain this sigil.|
</details>

<details>
<summary>6 New Activated Sigil(s):
</summary>

|Name			|Description	|
|-----------------------|---------------|
|Danse Macabre		| Activate: Pay 2 Bones to reverse this sigil effect. At the end of the owner's turn, all creatures on the board gain 1 health.|
|Dormant		| Activate: Pay Bones equal to the power of a card bearing this sigil to disable this sigil for 1 turn. a card bearing this sigil cannot attack normally.|
|Evocation		| Activate: Pay 3 Energy to return the oldest non-Brittle allied creature from the graveyard to your hand.|
|Life Draw		| Activate: Pay 1 Blood to draw a card from your main deck.|
|Soulgate		| Activate: Pay 1 Bone to gain 1 Energy, lose as much Max Energy at the end of the owner's turn.|
|Transmute		| Activate: Pay 2 Bones to transform a card bearing this sigil to its alternate form.|
</details>
